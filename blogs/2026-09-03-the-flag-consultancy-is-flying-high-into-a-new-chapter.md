Founded in 1998, South East-based The Flag Consultancy Ltd supplies, installs and maintains flags for events, ceremonies and public spaces across the UK. The business has worked on major national events, including the London Marathon, as well as supplying flags for television productions such as Netflix’s The Diplomat and Doctor Who .
