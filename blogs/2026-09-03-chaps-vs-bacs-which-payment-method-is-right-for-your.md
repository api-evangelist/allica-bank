Key takeaways CHAPS vs BACS usually comes down to timing, value, cost and payment risk. BACS is usually better suited to planned payments such as payroll, supplier runs and Direct Debit collections. CHAPS is designed for same-day, high-value and time-sensitive payments where a delay could have commercial consequences.
