Ryan Hayes, founder of Chain House Brewing , began brewing small 20-litre batches for friends and family nearly two decades ago while working full-time as an electrician. Today, the business supplies pubs and bars across the region and operates a popular city centre venue on Market Street that has become a hub for the local community.
